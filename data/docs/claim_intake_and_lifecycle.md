
# Claim Intake and Lifecycle

The claims platform accepts a claim through an internal portal or an integrated channel. A claim record receives a unique claim identifier and starts in the Submitted state. The intake process validates mandatory policy, claimant, incident date and supporting document information.

After intake validation, the case enters the Triage state. The case management team checks completeness, classifies the claim and assigns it to the appropriate processing queue. The claim lifecycle commonly includes Submitted, Triage, Assessment, Pending Approval, Approved, Rejected and Closed states.

The case owner is responsible for maintaining the claim record and ensuring that decisions and supporting evidence are recorded in the case history. Every material state transition must include an actor, timestamp and reason.
