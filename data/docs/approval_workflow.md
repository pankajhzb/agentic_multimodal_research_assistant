
# Approval Workflow

Claims requiring financial or exception approval are routed to the approval workflow. The workflow evaluates claim category, estimated amount, policy rules and the approval matrix to determine the required approval level.

A standard approval flow is: claim assessment, approval request creation, manager review, decision recording and notification to the case owner. Higher-value claims may require an additional senior approver. The workflow must prevent the same approval task from being completed twice.

Approval decisions are recorded with the approver identity, decision, timestamp and comments. If an approval is rejected, the claim returns to the assigned case owner for additional information or corrective action. Approval events are retained for audit and operational reporting.
